How much evidence does a certified miss rate need?

When a model closes security alerts, nobody sees the real ones it closed by mistake. Certifying that miss rate takes a fixed number of audited misses, and a public benchmark cannot supply it.

  • 24audited misses for one exact certificate
  • 55–77if it must hold at every moment
  • 2.4available from the benchmark at a 3% audit rate
  • 10×shortfall
  • 9.8to detect a miss rate at twice the budget
  • 89.2%miss rate a naive self-training triager drifts to

Limits. The formulas are classical; the audit-cost analysis and measurements are the contribution. Experiments use a logistic-regression scorer; the language-model tier is a small pilot.

Infrastructure time bombs.

  2. T2Hard expiry not renewed
  3. T3Cleanup of a referenced object

  5. T5Third-party end of life

  8. T8Silent stop

  • Agreement κ 0.89 / 0.91
  • 31 and 11 real cases of 200
  • Only 4 of 31 detectable from config
